## 1. Program facts

- Codename: Porchlight. The brand agent replaces it in Phase 2.

- Nostalgia window under study: 1993 to 1998 (decision D-003).
- Target audience: two generations. Parents born 1981 to 1990 (narrowed from 1992 by Phase 1 evidence). Children age 6 to 14. Details in `AUDIENCE.md`.

## 2. Block (canon from Phase 3, `projects/03-block-format/out/block-bible.md`)

- Fixed night: Friday. Encore window: Saturday and Sunday. A play in the encore window counts as a household return.
- Premiere clock time: one owner-set config constant, `PREMIERE_TIME`. It never moves. Not set at trailer stage.
- Four slots in a fixed order. The order never changes.

| Slot | Label | Content | Full-episode run time | v1 trailer length | Age lean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anchor | Multi-generation family sitcom | 22 min | 90 s | Both generations, tilting parent |
| 2 | Hangout | Tween and teen ensemble comedy | 22 min | 90 s | 10 to 14 |
| 3 | Wildcard | Sketch, variety, or odd animated comedy | 12 min | 60 to 75 s | 6 to 9 for the first laugh |
| 4 | Lights Out | Mild spooky anthology | 18 min | 90 to 120 s | 10 to 14, safe for 6 to 9 |

- Connective tissue at full-episode stage: up to 8 minutes.
- The label "Lights Out" stays. The stay-up-late framing is dropped from all v1 copy. No public asset says "stay up," "bedtime," or "past your bedtime."
- The block works with one lead show: one full episode plus three trailers or shorts under the same wrap.
- Trailer release cadence: all four trailers live from launch (decision D-017).

## 2a. Connective tissue

- Home base: a front porch at dusk. Three steps, a left-side railing, a screen door on a spring, a curtained window lit from inside, a hanging brass lamp with a pull chain, a tin mailbox with a red flag, a hand-lettered four-line card, a milk crate, a doormat with the brand word. No seating.
- The lamp is the block's visual identity. On means live. Off means Lights Out. Lamp color by slot: amber, multicolor string bulbs, a random settle from three colors, lantern only.
- Fallback home base if the final name fights the porch: a garage with the door half up and a work light on a hook. Lamp, card, mailbox, and Moth transfer unchanged.
- Sonic ID: a recorded pull-chain click, then four plucked notes. In variant unresolved, out variant resolved, Lights Out variant slow and low, ending on the click. No lyric at v1. Under 2 seconds. Brand brief adds: one real instrument, peaks no louder than dialogue.
- Host: one adult, an original character, not a cast member of any show. Working role name: the Porch Host. Recorded wrap only. Second person plural. Never says "kids." Never enters a story. No child on camera. No viewer submission read or shown. Name and casting in Phase 4. Casting triggers CG-04 (human) or CG-03 (AI-assisted).
- The Moth: a round, fuzzy animated moth that lands on the lamp shade. Silent at v1. An ident figure, not a co-host. Never inside an episode.
- Bumpers: the Click (3 s, out of every slot) and the Porch Intro (8 s, into every slot).
- Six interstitials at full-episode stage: the Sign-On, the Screen Door, Porch Games, the Moth Short, the Lantern, the After Porch. Porch Games generate no data.
- Crossovers: only after each show has played a full season, only in a stunt week, at most two per season, both showrunners sign. Setting crosses freely because all four shows share one town. Lights Out characters never enter the other three slots. The host never enters any story.
- Stunts per season: Lamp On (premiere), the Long Dusk (autumn), Snow Night (midwinter), Whole Street Night (finale, the only character crossover window). Summer: the Rerun Window.
- Trailer structure: cold open beat, world beat, character beat, turn, button, title card. Five tests: Question, Two-Laugh, Cold, Dialogue, Standards. A trailer that fails any test is recut.

## 3. Brand (provisional, Phase 2, `projects/02-brand/out/`)

- Name: not decided. Fork D open. The brand-strategist recommends "Streetlights" (`names-5.md`), marked `COUNSEL_GATE` CG-01. The site uses it as a provisional config constant.
- Voice: a confident host who is glad you came. Warm, plain, funny on purpose, never winking. Ten rules in `voice.md`.
- Promise to parents: one night a week, one couch, one thing you all laugh at, and nothing you have to apologize for.
- Promise to kids (spoken by the host on screen, never written as a call to action): something that is yours every week, with a little scare at the end and a safe landing.
- Public ritual words: [NAME] Night, the block, Anchor, Hangout, Wildcard, Lights Out, the Wrap, Home Base, the Last Word, the Listing (parent email), Save the night (button), I'd watch this (vote).
- Identity: Archivo (condensed width) plus Newsreader. Paper #F3ECDD, ink #1C1A16, accent #C9391E, glow #FFC46B, with a dark set. No textures, no shadows, no scan lines. Logo Direction 1: the masthead with one amber dot.

- The Returns Desk: fair-clue rule (cause written first, clue on screen, reveal is a replay), every reveal lands safe and funny, the receipts joke belongs to this show only.

## 5. Product (v1 trailer site, decision D-013)

- The site is a listings grid. One channel. At trailer stage: the Friday row with four slot cells, ordinals First to Last, no clock times. At full-episode stage: seven days, other days hold only the encore, the rerun, the Moth Short, the Host's Note, and the ritual kit. Empty cells stay empty. No fake networks or listings. The site never uses the name of any listings magazine.
- Each show opens to a trailer, a vote (I'd watch this / Not for us), and a parent sign-up. Parent-only. No child data. No comments. `noindex` until CG-02 and CG-03 clear.
- Measurement per trailer: play, 25, 50, 75, complete, vote, sign-up. Per household: return in a later week. Develop-further line: 500 parent sign-ups overall, and per show at least 40 percent completion and a vote ratio at or above 3 to 1.
- Stack: Cloudflare Workers, Hono, TypeScript, D1, Drizzle, wrangler. Code in `projects/05-product/site/`.

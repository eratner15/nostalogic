# STANDARDS

Content rules for the `standards` agent. Every script and every public asset gets a pass or fail report against this file. A single FAIL blocks release.

## 1. Audience floor and ceiling

- Safe for age 6 and up. Interesting for age 35 and up.
- The test: a 6-year-old can watch without fear, shame, or confusion. A 35-year-old parent laughs at least once per scene.

## 2. Hard FAIL items

Any one of these fails the asset.

1. Sexual content, innuendo aimed at kids, or romance beyond a first crush or a hand hold.
2. Graphic violence, blood, weapons used against people, or injury played for realism.
3. Profanity, slurs, or substitute words that read as profanity.
4. Alcohol, tobacco, vaping, drugs, or gambling shown as fun or normal.
5. Bullying, cruelty, or humiliation that the story rewards or leaves unanswered.
6. Adults who threaten children, or children in unsupervised danger played for laughs.
7. Horror that exceeds "mild spooky": no gore, no jump scares with real threat, no possession, no self-harm.
8. Real-world hate, politics, or religion as the target of a joke.
9. Product placement or sponsor mention inside a story without a clear label.
10. Any call to action aimed at a child: buy, click, subscribe, comment, share, or give information.
11. Any request for a child's name, age, location, image, or contact detail.
12. Copied expression from any existing show: character, set, theme song, logo, catchphrase, couch prop, or plot.
13. Use of the trademarks "TGIF" or "SNICK" in any public asset.
14. Imitation of a real actor's voice or likeness.
15. AI-generated performance, voice, or image without a logged counsel gate and a disclosure.

## 3. Soft rules (WARN, must be fixed before release)

1. Scares in Slot 4 resolve with safety and humor by the end of the segment.
2. Sarcasm from a child to a parent lands as affection, not contempt.
3. Body jokes stay at "gross," never at "shame."
4. Every episode has one moment a parent and a child can talk about after.
5. Screen time inside the story is neutral. Never a punchline about "kids these days."
6. Families on screen vary in shape. No family shape is a joke.
7. Food scenes show ordinary food. No diet talk.
8. Sound levels: no sudden loud effect louder than the dialogue peak.
9. Flashing lights: no strobe faster than 3 flashes per second.

## 4. Public asset rules

1. No em dashes in any human-facing text.
2. Every public page states that the account holder is a parent.
3. No comments, no personalized ads, no child login, no child data.
4. Every claim about audience or results cites a source or a metric.

## 5. Report format

```
ASSET: <path>
RESULT: PASS | FAIL
FAIL ITEMS: <numbered list from Section 2, with line or timestamp>
WARN ITEMS: <numbered list from Section 3, with line or timestamp>
NOTES: <at most 5 lines>
```

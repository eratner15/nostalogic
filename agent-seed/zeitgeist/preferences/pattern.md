# The Mahjong pattern (read-only)

A trend fits the pattern when it has most of these layers:

1. Analog or in-person activity done in groups.
2. A learning curve, so beginners need a guide.
3. A gear and aesthetic layer (sets, mats, accessories, design).
4. A host layer (parties, clubs, classes, events).
5. An identity layer (people say "I play", not "I tried it").

## Lanes

- Lane A, adjacent to the book: trends the mahjong buyer also buys. Seed list
  to test, not to assume: canasta, bridge, rummikub, card-game nights, puzzle
  clubs, supper clubs, needlework and crafts, game-night hosting.
- Lane B, open field: any rising trend in culture, commerce, or behavior that
  fits the pattern, including nostalgia waves and analog revivals.

## Signals (use at least two per trend)

- Search demand: Google Trends, 5-year and 90-day views.
- Book demand: Amazon Best Sellers and Movers & Shakers by category.
- Product demand: Etsy, Amazon, Kickstarter.
- Social attention: TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest Trends, Reddit growth.
- Event demand: Eventbrite, Meetup, Partiful, local news.
- Press: NYT Style, WSJ, Vogue, Axios, trade press.
- Money: funding, retail buyer, and licensing news.

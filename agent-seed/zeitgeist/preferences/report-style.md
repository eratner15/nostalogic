# Report style (read-only)

Reader: Evan, deciding which 1-3 trends to test next. Write for that decision.

Sections, in order:
1. Top line: three sentences. What changed, the best move, the risk.
2. Trend board: table of name, lane, stage, score, band, change since last run.
3. Top 3 GO trends: evidence with links, factor scores, revenue ladder steps,
   and one test for the next 14 days with its cost and its stop signal.
4. Book line moves: next actions for the mahjong book itself.
5. Fading list: trends that dropped a band, and what to stop.
6. Unread sources: each source you could not read, with the reason, or
   "All sources read."

Revenue ladder, bottom first: book line, printables, gear, email list and
community, classes and events, licensing and B2B, media.

Plain short sentences. No em dashes. No hype. Mark fact versus read.

# Scoring rubric (read-only)

Revival Readiness Score = 0.30 x social_buzz + 0.40 x window alignment + 0.30 x modern_relevance.

- Window alignment comes from the year: the original fans' age now against a
  40-year-old sweet spot. You cannot propose it; it moves with the calendar.
- social_buzz is computed from Wikipedia pageviews and subreddit activity
  (rubric r3). You cannot propose it.
- A property is flagged when its weekly score reaches 80. A flag is a hit if
  revival news (announced or released) arrives within 24 months.

## Fields you can propose (0-100 unless noted)

- modern_relevance: how well the property's themes fit audiences today.
- rights_complexity: higher = harder to clear (many holders, disputes,
  estates). Lower it only when the source shows rights consolidating.
- creator_availability: higher = original creators are active and willing.
- current_signal: one line of text describing the newest real signal.

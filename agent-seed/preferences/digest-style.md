# Digest style (read-only)

- Reader: Evan, deciding which properties deserve development or rights
  diligence. Write for that decision.
- 15 lines at most in summary_md. Short plain sentences. No em dashes.
- Name each property with its year, for example "Daria (1997)".
- Separate fact (quoted from a source) from your reading of it.
- Last line: the sources you could not read, or "All sources read."

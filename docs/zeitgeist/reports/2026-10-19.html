Fading list and watch-outs

This is the first run, so no trend dropped a band. These signals need watching:

  • Mahjong plateau. The "mahjong starter kit" Amazon keyword dropped 7,449 positions in one week in July (asinsight). Searches for clubs still hit highs in August.
  • Cultural appropriation backlash. The Boston Globe covered it in March 2026. Credit the game's Chinese and Jewish-American history in all products.
  • Adult Pokemon. Big but speculative, and the buyer is far from yours. Do not chase it with the mahjong brand.
  • Run clubs. At peak, crowded, and a poor fit. Stop considering it.
  • NostalDamus tie-in. Pokemon and Tamagotchi both turn 30 in 2026. A "1996 anniversary" brief is a NostalDamus product, not a book-line product.

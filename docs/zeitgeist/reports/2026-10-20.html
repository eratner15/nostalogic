What must be true for "super premium, word of mouth"
  • The host sells it. The box is bought by one woman for her table of six. Every guest leaves with a card that says where it came from. That is the word of mouth.
  • A design no one else has. Mahjong tiles in needlepoint and Tila beads are yours. A generic floral kit competes with 345,419 listings at a $35 median.
  • Real photos of real finished work. None exist yet. The stitched sample is the first.
  • Honest reviews only. Do not give boxes in exchange for reviews. Etsy's review rules could not be read this run, so check them before any launch offer.
  • The price must survive Offsite Ads at 12% for ever. All three boxes do; punch needle only barely.
  • Packaging that feels like a gift, because the buyer is giving the night to her friends.
Etsy policy flags
  • Food is out. Under "Sourced by a seller", Etsy no longer allows "Resold generic cooking tools or ingredients" (June 2025 update). A charcuterie or board-night kit is exactly that. (Value Added Resource, Jun 2025)
  • No resold ready-to-use blanks. Same update: "Resold blank items that are ready to use without craft intervention" are not allowed. A kit must need the buyer's own making.
  • Party favours and holiday decor were removed from the allowed "Sourced by a seller" examples. Do not sell the host card or table decor as a separate item.
  • Photos must be your own, or the maker's or distributor's commercial images.
  • A curated box you assembled counts as "Handpicked by a seller", and production partners must be listed on the shop page (Listadum, 20 Jul 2026, a third-party reading of Etsy's rules). Etsy's own text was blocked; confirm the kit category in the listing form before publishing.
  • Shop rules still apply: no invented reviews, and never say a bag holds a full mahjong set.

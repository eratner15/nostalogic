02 · The product

A pet that thinks for itself
and never talks to the internet.

BrainA tiny language model (3–5M parameters) on a $20 ESP32-S3 board with 8 MB PSRAM
PrivacyMicrophone removed or switched off. No cloud. No account. Works forever.
BuyerAdults 32–45 who had a virtual pet in 1997. No children in 2026.
Price$129 Founders Edition · $99 gift box · $149 Developer Edition (50, inside the 300)
Refills$12 species packs (the "sticker paper" of this product)
OpenModel, firmware, case files on Hugging Face and GitHub
ClosedName, characters, species packs. This is what a buyer buys.
Concept only · touch screen and side buttons, board picked Oct 27; a pebble, never an egg with three buttons (avoids Bandai trade dress)2 / 9

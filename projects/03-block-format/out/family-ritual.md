# Family Ritual

Project Porchlight, Phase 3. Agent: format-architect. Prepared 2026-09-19. Standalone version of `block-bible.md` section 7.

One household, invented for this page. The Amadi-Kowalczyk household: one parent, Rosa, and two children, Tomasz, age 7, and Ify, age 12. Any resemblance to a real family is accidental. The brand name is not set, so this page writes `[BRAND]`. The fixed night is Friday. The premiere clock time is the owner's config constant `PREMIERE_TIME` and is not stated here.

Every ritual element on this page is original. None of it asks a child for anything. Rosa is the account holder. The site never learns Tomasz or Ify exist.

## Trailer stage (v1)

### Before

1. Wednesday. The Host's Note lands in Rosa's email. It carries the four-line card for Friday, one question, and the name of the show that premieres next. It is the only reminder. No platform notification exists for made-for-kids content, so the reminder lives in the parent's inbox.
2. Thursday. Rosa opens the ritual kit from the Thursday cell on the grid. One printable page: the card with a box to tick per show, a snack card (popcorn in a mixing bowl), and the question. She prints it or leaves it on her phone. Ify tapes the card to the fridge.
3. Friday morning. The parent email lands with `PREMIERE_TIME` in the subject line and the card in the body.

### During

1. After dinner, Rosa opens `[BRAND]` in the living room TV browser. The grid comes up. One channel. Friday is full.
2. Tomasz presses the Sign-On. The pull chain clicks. The lamp comes on. Four notes. The host reads the card. The screen door opens.
3. The family watches tonight's premiere trailer inside its Porch Intro and its Click. If earlier trailers are live, they watch those in slot order first: Anchor, Hangout, Wildcard, Lights Out.
4. After each Click, the vote appears. The copy addresses Rosa: "Parents: would your house watch this?" Tomasz and Ify argue. Rosa holds the remote and taps what the house decides. One tap. No name. No data about the kids.
5. The whole sitting is under ten minutes.

### After

1. The Sign-Off gives the question: "Which house on that street would you knock on first?" They answer out loud. Ify says Lights Out. Tomasz says Wildcard. Rosa says the Anchor and gets booed.
2. Rosa signs up for the note with her email, if she has not. The page states the account holder is a parent.
3. Saturday or Sunday, if Ify's friend missed Friday, that household watches the encore cell. It counts as a return.
4. Wednesday. The note. Next Friday's card. The ritual restarts.

## Full-episode stage

### Before

1. Wednesday. The Host's Note: the card, the question, and a still from the close-up box.
2. Thursday. The ritual kit. Ify ticks the show she is waiting for on the fridge card. Tomasz draws on the lamp with four dots under it, which is the sonic ID printed as a doodle.
3. Friday morning. The email with `PREMIERE_TIME`.
4. Friday dinner is whatever it is. The snack waits for the Anchor credits.

### During

1. The Sign-On. Lamp on. The host reads the card and opens the screen door.
2. Anchor. Rosa laughs first. Tomasz laughs at the kid who turns out to be right.
3. The Screen Door into Hangout. Ify sits up. The multicolor bulbs come on along the railing.
4. Porch Games. Freeze when the lamp flickers. Tomasz cheats. Nobody sends anything anywhere.
5. The Moth Short. Thirty seconds of silent comedy at the lamp.
6. Wildcard. The lamp lands on a color. Tomasz repeats the bit for the rest of the night.
7. The Lantern. The lamp goes off. The host lights the lantern. Rosa asks Tomasz if he is in or out. This is the exit cue for a 6 to 9 household. He is in, on her lap.
8. Lights Out. The hush. The scare. The safe landing. The laugh. The lamp comes back on.

### After

1. The After Porch. The host asks the one question. The family talks for two minutes. Then everybody moves.
2. Rosa taps "we watched" on the grid. That tap is the strongest return signal the site ever gets from this household.
3. The host puts next week's card in the mailbox. The flag goes up.
4. Saturday and Sunday, the encore cells hold the block for any household that missed Friday.
5. Wednesday. The note. The card. Again.

## What the ritual never includes

- A child login, a child name, a child photo, a child comment, or a child vote.
- A platform notification. The only reminder is the parent email.
- A "stay up late" or "past your bedtime" line anywhere. The label Lights Out stays. The framing does not.
- A prize, a contest, a mail-in, or a submission. Porch Games are played in the room and stay in the room.
- Any set, prop, song, catchphrase, or character from any existing show.

## The objects the ritual runs on

| Object | Where it lives | Job |
|---|---|---|
| The card | Porch mailbox, kit, email, grid | The same four lines everywhere. The family's checklist. |
| The lamp | Porch, bumpers, kit doodle | On means live. Off means Lights Out. |
| The four notes | Sign-On, bumpers, Sign-Off, kit doodle | The thing a parent hums on Thursday. |
| The question | After Porch, note, kit | The one thing to talk about after (STANDARDS soft 4). |
| The snack card | Kit | Ordinary food. No diet talk (STANDARDS soft 7). |
| The "we watched" tap | Grid, full-episode stage | The household return signal. Parent only. |

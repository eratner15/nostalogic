# Canon Proposal: Block Format

Project Porchlight, Phase 3. Agent: format-architect. Prepared 2026-09-19.
For the Orchestrator. Proposed additions to `canon/CANON.md` section 2 (Block) and a new section 2a (Connective tissue). Source: `projects/03-block-format/out/block-bible.md`. The format-architect does not edit `canon/`.

## Proposed facts for CANON.md section 2, Block

1. The fixed night is Friday. Reason and runner-up are in `block-bible.md` section 2. Scorecard row 1, Reproduce.
2. The premiere clock time is one owner-set config constant, `PREMIERE_TIME`. It never moves. Canon records the constant's name, not a value. Scorecard row 7, Adapt.
3. The encore window is Saturday and Sunday. A play in the encore window counts as a household return.
4. Four slots in a fixed order: Anchor, Hangout, Wildcard, Lights Out. The order never changes. Scorecard row 3, Adapt.
5. Full-episode run times: Anchor 22 minutes, Hangout 22 minutes, Wildcard 12 minutes, Lights Out 18 minutes. Connective tissue up to 8 minutes.
6. v1 trailer lengths: Anchor 90 seconds, Hangout 90 seconds, Wildcard 60 to 75 seconds, Lights Out 90 to 120 seconds.
7. Age lean per slot: Anchor both generations tilting parent; Hangout 10 to 14; Wildcard 6 to 9 for the first laugh; Lights Out 10 to 14 and safe for 6 to 9 by design.
8. The label "Lights Out" stays. The stay-up-late framing is dropped from all v1 copy. No public asset says "stay up," "bedtime," or "past your bedtime." Scorecard row 10, Drop for v1.
9. The block works with one lead show: one full episode plus three trailers or shorts under the same wrap. The grid marks the other slots "coming to this slot."

## Proposed facts for a new CANON.md section 2a, Connective tissue

10. Home base: a front porch at dusk. Objects: three steps, a left-side railing, a screen door on a spring, a curtained window lit from inside, a hanging brass lamp with a pull chain, a tin mailbox with a red flag, a hand-lettered four-line card, a milk crate, a doormat with the brand word. No seating. Full description in `block-bible.md` 4.2. Scorecard row 2, Adapt.
11. The lamp is the block's visual identity. On means live. Off means Lights Out. Lamp color by slot: amber, multicolor string bulbs, a random settle from three colors, and lantern only.
12. Fallback home base if Fork D produces a name that fights the porch: a garage with the door half up and a work light on a hook. The lamp, card, mailbox, and Moth transfer unchanged.
13. Sonic ID: a recorded pull-chain click, then four plucked notes. In variant unresolved, out variant resolved, Lights Out variant slow and low ending on the click. No lyric at v1. Under 2 seconds. Scorecard row 6, Adapt.
14. Host: one adult, an original character, not a cast member of any show, working role name "the Porch Host." Recorded wrap only. Addresses the household in the second person plural. Never says "kids." Never enters a show's story. No child on camera. No viewer submission read or shown. Name and casting come in Phase 4. Scorecard row 8, Test. Measured by Sign-On plays against slot plays.
15. Host performance path triggers a counsel gate either way: CG-04 for human talent, CG-03 for AI-assisted.
16. The Moth: a round, fuzzy animated moth that lands on the lamp shade. Silent at v1. An ident figure, not a co-host. May appear in bumpers and the Moth Short, never inside an episode.
17. Bumpers: the Click (3 seconds, out of every slot) and the Porch Intro (8 seconds, into every slot). Scorecard row 4, Adapt.
18. Six interstitials at full-episode stage: the Sign-On, the Screen Door, Porch Games, the Moth Short, the Lantern, the After Porch. Porch Games generate no data. Scorecard row 4.
19. Crossover rules: allowed only after each show has played a full season, only in a stunt week, at most two per season, both showrunners sign. Setting crosses freely because all four shows share one town. Lights Out characters never enter the other three slots. The host never enters any story. Scorecard row 9, Test, gated to phase F4.
20. Four stunts per season: Lamp On (premiere), the Long Dusk (autumn, Lights Out double, lamp off all night), Snow Night (midwinter, shared snow day, setting crossover), Whole Street Night (finale, the only character crossover window). Summer: the Rerun Window. Scorecard row 5, Adapt.
21. Event calendar by phase: T1 trailer launch, T2 trailer run, T3 develop-further call, F1 lead show block, F2 two to four shows, F3 first full season, F4 second season with crossovers if qualified.

## Proposed facts for CANON.md section 5, Product (format inputs only)

22. The site is a listings grid. One channel, seven days. The fixed night row holds the Sign-On cell and four slot cells. Other days hold only the encore, the rerun, the Moth Short, the Host's Note, and the ritual kit. Empty cells stay empty. No fake networks or listings. The site never uses the name of any listings magazine.
23. The card is the same object on the porch, in the kit, in the email, and on the grid.
24. Trailer structure: cold open beat, world beat, character beat, turn, button, title card. Five tests: Question, Two-Laugh, Cold, Dialogue, Standards. A trailer that fails any test is recut.
25. Measurement events per trailer: play (10 seconds), q25, q50, q75, complete, vote_watch, vote_skip, signup, signup source. Per block night: signon_play, order adherence, encore_play, household return, we_watched. No child data. CG-02 reviews the list.
26. Develop-further line (D-013 restated): 500 parent sign-ups overall, and per show at least 40 percent completion and a vote ratio at or above 3 to 1.

## Decision the Orchestrator must log

27. Trailer release cadence. Option A: all four trailers live from launch, the fixed night changes only the wrap. Option B: all four shows on the grid from launch, one premieres per fixed night in slot order, all four stay live after the fourth night. The format-architect recommends B. Reason: it gives the sign-up and the fixed night a reason, and makes the week 4 return rate measure four returns. Tradeoff: week 1 visitors see one trailer. D-013's wording implies A, so this needs a logged decision.

## Glossary additions proposed for `canon/GLOSSARY.md`

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Encore | The Saturday and Sunday on-demand window after the fixed night. A play in it counts as a return. |
| Card | The hand-lettered four-line listing for one block night. Appears on the porch, in the kit, in the email, and on the grid. |
| Lamp | The porch lamp. The block's visual identity. On means live. Off means Lights Out. |
| Sign-On | The host's opening wrap on the fixed night. |
| Sign-Off | The host's closing wrap, with the question and next week's card. |
| Grid | The one-channel, seven-day listings that serve as the site's navigation. |
| Note | The Host's Note. The mid-week parent email and page. |

# Names: 30 candidates

Project Porchlight, Phase 2. Brand strategist output. Internal.

Rules applied to every candidate:
- No use or echo of the two block trademarks named in `canon/STANDARDS.md` section 2, item 13, or of any existing block brand.
- No "TV" plus a day of the week.
- No known existing network, block, app, or streaming brand.
- "Porchlight" is the codename and is excluded. Candidates in the same emotional family (home, evening, gathering, light, a signal that says "come in") are welcome.
- Registers: warm, playful, mysterious, retro, modern. Most names carry two.
- "N" marks a network-style name: short, sayable, chantable by a kid. Ten candidates carry the mark. The task asked for eight.

## Selection for the screen

The five that go to `names-5.md`: Streetlights, Rumpus, Pilot Light, Screen Door, Lights Up.

Why these five:
1. Each carries the light or come-in signal without saying "porch."
2. Each has a double meaning a parent gets and a kid does not need.
3. Each reads premium on a printed masthead, not kitsch.
4. Two are network-style (Streetlights, Rumpus). Three are two-word marks with strong logo shapes.

Held in reserve if all five fail counsel: Lamppost, Hall Light, Gloaming, Homeglow, Evenglow.

# LONG ROUTE. Character sheet.

Hangout slot (Slot 2). Visual descriptions for an image generation model and for casting. Staff-writer output, Phase 4.

Purpose: keep faces, costumes, props, and frames consistent across every shot. Every person below is original. None is modeled on a real actor or an existing character. Casting notes do not use "like" comparisons to real people, per the bible section 9, item 14.

Use of this sheet under CG-03: any AI-generated child likeness in released material requires the logged counsel gate and the disclosure (bible section 11). At trailer stage, plan human child performers for dialogue and use this sheet for plates, composites, and continuity.

Format for each regular: age, build, face, hair, skin, constant costume pieces, the one prop, posture, and the fixed frame (seat and camera angle).

---

## The regulars

### Frances Dukes. The driver.

- **Age.** 58.
- **Build.** Medium height. Sturdy. Broad in the shoulders from thirty years of a wheel and an upright bass. Not slim, not heavy. Strong forearms.
- **Face.** Square jaw. Gray eyes with deep crow's feet she does not use. A long straight nose. Mouth set flat, the corners ready to move and choosing not to. Reading glasses on a cord, pushed up into her hair, never on her face.
- **Hair.** Short silver, cut close at the neck, longer on top, a cowlick at the crown that stands up by the ridge.
- **Skin.** Light, weathered, freckled on the forearms and the backs of the hands. A driver's tan on the left arm only.
- **Costume.** A quilted navy vest over a plaid flannel shirt (red and black, or green and black, by season) with the sleeves rolled to the elbow exactly twice. A county driver ID on a lanyard tucked into the vest pocket so only the clip shows. Brown leather half-gloves in snow. Work boots.
- **Prop.** A steel thermos with a dented lid, seat-belted into row 1 with the clipboard.
- **Posture.** Upright. Both hands on the wheel at nine and three. Chin level. Eyes up to the mirror, not to the road, when she speaks. She never turns her head to talk to a rider.
- **Fixed frame.** The driver's seat. Her portrait is the mirror: a tight reverse shot of her eyes in the wide convex mirror above the windshield, the aisle bent small behind them. She is the only regular whose frame contains all the others.

### Hollis Dukes. Row 1, door side. The driver's grandson.

- **Age.** 13. Small for it.
- **Build.** Slight. Narrow shoulders. All elbows. Sits low in the seat and takes up less room than his backpack.
- **Face.** Long and thin, big ears that the hood is meant to hide, brown eyes that are always a little too wide, a mouth that apologizes before it opens. Freckles across the nose.
- **Hair.** Light brown, straight, cut by his grandmother, sticks up at the back of the crown no matter what.
- **Skin.** Light, with a fading summer tan that is gone by episode 5.
- **Costume.** A gray hooded sweatshirt with the hood always half up, never all the way. A faded blue nylon windbreaker over it, unzipped. Canvas sneakers with the laces double-knotted. The windbreaker is the constant; the hood position tells you his mood.
- **Prop.** A yellow pencil behind his right ear. It fixes the door. He has never said how.
- **Posture.** Braced. Both hands on the chrome rail of the seat back in front of him, feet flat, shoulders up around his ears, whether the bus is moving or not.
- **Fixed frame.** Row 1, door side. Framed against the door glass and the folding door, so the stop is always behind him. Camera from the stairwell, low, at his eye height, looking slightly up. Frances's shoulder and the thermos are visible at the left edge of frame.

### Mateo Villanueva. Row 12, driver side. Speaker of the parliament.

- **Age.** 14.
- **Build.** Tall and lanky, the tallest on the bus by a head, all wrists and knees. Grew over the summer and has not caught up to it.
- **Face.** Long. Heavy dark eyebrows that do most of his talking. Dark brown eyes. A wide mouth held closed. A chin he has started to lift when he is pretending not to care.
- **Hair.** Black, thick, a little long on top, pushed back with a hand and never a comb. Short at the sides.
- **Skin.** Warm medium brown. Sun on the back of his neck from the lane.
- **Costume.** A faded green canvas work jacket, too big, that was his uncle's. A plain white T-shirt. Brown work boots with dried mud on the outer sides from the far-end lane. The jacket is the constant.
- **Prop.** A key ring with no keys on it, for the car his cousin is getting. He turns it on one finger.
- **Posture.** Sideways across the back-row seat, back to the window, one knee up on the vinyl, arms folded, head against the glass. He does not sit facing forward unless something has gone wrong.
- **Fixed frame.** Row 12, driver side. Framed against the rear driver-side window with the road falling away behind him. Camera from the aisle at seat height, a hair below his eyeline, so the back row looks like the top of something.

### Beck Oyelaran. Row 12, door side. The whip.

- **Age.** 12.
- **Build.** Medium height, compact, springy. Never fully still. Leans forward from the hips.
- **Face.** Round, open, a wide grin with a lot of teeth, big expressive eyebrows that go up before he stands. Dark brown eyes. A face that cannot hold a secret and is proud of it.
- **Hair.** Short high-top fade with a straight part shaved on the left side.
- **Skin.** Dark brown, warm, with a glow that the road plates catch.
- **Costume.** A bright striped long-sleeve rugby shirt, orange and navy. A navy puffer vest with hand-made pins on it. High-top sneakers, red. The vest and the pins are the constant. The shirt stripes may change color by episode but always stay bold.
- **Prop.** A hand-lettered campaign button on the vest, the size of a jar lid, that changes each episode. Pilot: BECK FOR SPEAKER. He makes them himself and the lettering shows it.
- **Posture.** Half-standing, one foot in the aisle, one hand on the grab rail of the seat in front like a podium. When he is seated all the way it is because Priya said so.
- **Fixed frame.** Row 12, door side. Framed against the rear door-side window. Camera from the aisle, low, so that when he stands he fills the frame to the roof.

### Rue Oyelaran. Row 11, door side. The swing vote.

- **Age.** 12. Beck's twin.
- **Build.** Same height as Beck, thinner, and completely still. Long neck. Sits with her weight over the sketchbook.
- **Face.** A long oval. Beck's eyebrows, held flat. Deep brown eyes that look up before the head does. A small mouth that moves only for the one line.
- **Hair.** Shoulder-length two-strand twists pulled back with a single black elastic, a few twists loose at the temples.
- **Skin.** Dark brown, the same tone as Beck. The two are clearly twins and clearly not the same person.
- **Costume.** An oversized olive cardigan with the sleeves pulled over her hands. A plain T-shirt under it, any muted color. A canvas messenger bag worn across her chest that does not come off on the bus. The cardigan and the bag are the constant.
- **Prop.** A hardbound black sketchbook with a wide rubber band around it and a mechanical pencil clipped to the band.
- **Posture.** Turned toward the window, sketchbook on her knees, chin down, eyes up. She draws the school through the glass without looking at the page.
- **Fixed frame.** Row 11, door side. Framed in three-quarter profile against her window, the road plate sharp behind her and Beck's shoulder soft in the foreground. Camera from the aisle at seat height.

### Cal Whitlock. Row 7, driver side. The market.

- **Age.** 13.
- **Build.** Stocky, broad, strong from stacking crates at the store. Takes up the whole seat and the hump under it.
- **Face.** Round and ruddy. Small sharp blue eyes that count. A gap between the front teeth. Cheeks that go pink in the cold before anyone else's.
- **Hair.** Sandy red-blond, cut short and flat on top.
- **Skin.** Pale, freckled, quick to burn in blossom season.
- **Costume.** A forest-green fleece quarter-zip with WHITLOCK'S stitched in white over the heart. Khaki cargo pants with every pocket in use. A cheap black digital watch. The fleece is the constant.
- **Prop.** The ledger: a green clothbound account book, the kind a store keeps, with a pen clipped to the cover and paper tabs sticking out of the side.
- **Posture.** Feet up on the wheel hump, ledger on his knees, leaning out into the aisle so he can see both ends of the bus at once.
- **Fixed frame.** Row 7, driver side. Framed against the mid-bus window, slightly wider than the others to show the hump and his feet on it. Camera from the aisle at seat height. The middle of the market.

### Priya Anand-Coker. Row 2, door side. Author of the bus code.

- **Age.** 11.
- **Build.** Small. The smallest person on the bus. Sits with a straight back that adds an inch.
- **Face.** Heart-shaped. Large dark eyes. Thick straight eyebrows. A serious mouth that is only ever surprised on the inside. She looks at people over the top of the composition book.
- **Hair.** Black, in one thick braid down her back, tight and neat, finished with a fabric scrunchie that changes color by episode.
- **Skin.** Warm medium brown.
- **Costume.** A navy cardigan buttoned to the top button. A white collared shirt under it. A backpack with every zipper zipped, worn on both shoulders even while seated. The cardigan and the two-shoulder backpack are the constant.
- **Prop.** The bus code: a black-and-white marbled composition book with BUS CODE in careful marker on the cover and colored paper tabs down the edge, one per section.
- **Posture.** Forward on the seat, feet flat, book open on her lap, pencil up. She never turns around when she cites. The back row hears her from the front.
- **Fixed frame.** Row 2, door side. Framed against the front door-side window, with the edge of Frances's shoulder and the mirror visible at the top left. Camera from the aisle, slightly above her eyeline. She is the only regular shot from above. It is the show's joke, and she is fighting it.

## Recurring faces seen in the trailer and the cold open

Short entries for continuity only. These are not regulars.

### Mr. Tull. The county's transportation man.

- 50s. Tall and thin, so the back-row seat folds him. A long neutral face, pale, clean-shaven, thinning gray-brown hair combed flat. Wire glasses. A plain tie (maroon), a white short-sleeve shirt, a county lanyard, gray slacks, black shoes that were shined this morning. The prop: a silver stopwatch on a black cord around his neck. Posture: knees up, elbows in, taking up as little room as a tall man can. He is never drawn as a threat. He looks like a man who would like to be told where to sit.

### Ines Villanueva. Mateo's sister.

- 6. Small and solid. Round face, Mateo's eyebrows in miniature, huge dark eyes, a gap where a front tooth was. Black hair in two short pigtails. Warm medium brown skin. A purple backpack bigger than her torso, a yellow raincoat in every weather. No prop; her whole body is the wave. Posture: on tiptoe at the mailbox, both arms up. She is only ever framed from the bus, through the windshield or the door, at the mailbox.

### Abuela Villanueva.

- 70s. Short, round, a cardigan over a housedress, silver hair in a low bun, warm brown skin, a mug in one hand. Seen on the porch at the far end, through the windshield. One wave.

### Ms. Odum. Teacher on bus duty.

- 40s. Medium height, a green quilted jacket over a school lanyard, a clipboard, brown hair in a low ponytail, brown skin, a tired smile. Seen only at the open door in the loop, from the door shot.

## The bus interior, for an artist

- **Vehicle.** A full-size county school bus, yellow, with a single black stripe along both sides and a stenciled black 12 on both sides and on the rear emergency door. No maker's name anywhere.
- **Layout.** Twelve rows. Two seats per row. A center aisle wide enough for one kid and Beck's foot. Green vinyl seats with cracked cream piping. Chrome grab rails along every seat back. Black rubber floor with a raised ribbed strip down the center of the aisle.
- **Fixtures.** A heater box under row 3, door side, painted the same green as the seats. Wheel humps under rows 6 and 7, both sides. A rear emergency door with a red handle and a stenciled warning that nobody touches. A wide convex mirror above the windshield with a chrome rim.
- **Row 1, the kitchen.** Driver side: the thermos, the clipboard, a laminated route card clipped to the seat back, a coffee can of pencils, and on some days an upright bass in a canvas case with the seat belt around it. Door side: Hollis.
- **Seats with value.** Row 3, door side: the heater seat. Rows 6 and 7: the hump seats. Row 9, door side: the Tuesday window, the only window latch that slides smooth. Row 9, driver side: the crack seat, a split in the vinyl holding folded notes. Row 12: the back row and the aisle between the two seats, where parliament sits.
- **The visor.** Above Frances, on the driver side, with the corner of a county envelope tucked behind the elastic. Present in every mirror shot. Never opened in season 1 before episode 10.
- **Light.** Morning: low warm light from the door side. Afternoon: low warm light from the driver side. The interior is never dark. Overhead lights are on only at the rail crossing and in snow.
- **Palette.** Bus yellow, seat green, chrome, black rubber, the red of the emergency handle. Nothing else bright except the kids.

## The four camera positions

1. **The mirror shot.** From above and behind Frances, as if through the convex mirror: a wide, slightly bent view down the aisle, every rider in one image, the rear door small at the end. The show's signature frame.
2. **The stairwell shot.** From the door steps looking back up the aisle, low, at kid height. Hollis nearest, the back row far.
3. **The seat rigs.** Six fixed portrait frames, one per assigned seat, described above under each regular. The road plate behind the window changes with the stop and the season. Nothing else in the frame changes.
4. **The door shot.** From inside the bus at the top of the steps, looking out the open door at whatever the stop brings: Ines, Ms. Odum, the coffee, the dog at the property line.

A handheld covers parliament in session from the aisle at row 10.

## The exterior road plates, for an artist

Every window is a plate. The plate tells the viewer where on the route we are without a line. Plates are organized by stop and by season. Each entry below is the base plate in early autumn (the pilot). Season variants follow.

1. **The bus barn.** A gravel lot at the edge of town. A long open-fronted metal shed with six bays, five yellow buses nose-out, a dispatch window with a lit desk lamp and a radio antenna. Chain-link at the back. Dawn: gray-blue, one sodium light still on.
2. **The far-end lane, Pennock Hollow.** A long gravel lane rising to a white dairy house with a deep porch. Wood fence on both sides. Four farms visible across a shallow valley. A mailbox at the top of the lane with a red flag and a small girl beside it. Dawn: pink light on the ridge behind, mist in the low field.
3. **The orchard, Hale Orchard stand.** Rows of apple trees on both sides of the road, crowns just touching over the ditch. A roadside stand with a shingle roof, wooden bins, and a chalkboard on an easel facing the road. A brown-and-white dog sitting exactly at a fence post. Morning: long shadows across the road.
4. **The creek bridge.** A one-lane steel truss bridge with wood planking. Willows on the left bank. A wide slow creek, brown-green, with a gravel bar. A gray heron standing on the bar, always in the same spot, left of the bridge. Morning: light on the water.
5. **The rail crossing.** Two tracks on a raised bed, a crossbuck sign, a gate arm up, gravel shoulders, scrub on both sides, the rails running straight out of frame both ways. Nothing on the tracks. Ever. Morning: flat light, no shadows.
6. **The crossroads, Whitlock's Corner.** A two-story white clapboard store with a covered porch, two gas pumps that are never in use, a letter-board sign on a pole with movable black letters, a four-way stop. Pop Whitlock in the doorway with a paper cup. Morning: the store lights on, the sky brightening.
7. **The ridge, the Look.** The road tops a rise and the whole town appears below for one curve: a grid of streets, a water tower, the school's flat roof, the church steeple far off, rooftops in the trees. This is the shared town of all four shows, seen from above. Morning: the town in full sun, the bus in shadow.
8. **Fieldstone.** A subdivision entrance with a low stone sign and new sod. A cul-de-sac visible beyond it with six identical houses and no trees yet. A parked sedan with a man inside doing a crossword. Morning: bright, flat, no shade.
9. **The school loop, North Union Consolidated.** From Position 3: a two-story brick school with a flat roof, a marquee sign on the lawn missing a letter, the gym's double doors, a row of second-floor windows (the eighth-grade window is the third from the left), a playground on the right. Buses ahead and behind. A crossing guard in an orange vest. Morning: kids in the windows. Afternoon: kids at the doors.
10. **The town detour.** Not in the pilot. Storefronts, porches, a streetlamp, the same town seen from street level. Reserved for episode 8.

### Season variants

- **Harvest.** Orchard bins full, red and gold trees, bags of apples on the stand, the chalkboard crowded. The far-end fields cut to stubble.
- **Rain.** The lane is mud, the creek is high and brown, the bridge plates are wet, the store porch has a puddle, the windows streak. Fog at the ridge blanks the town.
- **Snow.** Everything white, the road two tire tracks, chains on the bus, the heron gone, the barn lot plowed into a wall, the school loop scraped and salted. Frost on the inside of the windows.
- **Thaw.** Brown fields, running ditches, the creek up to the planks, the heron back on a smaller bar. Gray sky, bright ground.
- **Blossom.** The orchard white and pink over both shoulders of the road, petals on the windshield, the twins mortified. The far-end fields green. The ridge view hazy with pollen.

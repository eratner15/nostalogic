# KITCHEN COURT: 90-second teaser

Shooting script. Anchor slot. Project Porchlight, Phase 4.
Source: `projects/04-writers-room/out/bibles/A2-kitchen-court.md` section 12 (brief) and `projects/03-block-format/out/block-bible.md` section 8 (six beats, five tests).
Frames F1 to F6 are the fixed frames in the bible, section 11.

Production rules for this file:

- No narration. No text on screen until the title card.
- No music under the cold open. Music enters at 00:12, low, plucked, and drops out under the button.
- The Stool scrape, the two taps, the toast pop, and the whistle are mixed at or below the dialogue peak.
- The trailer is its own cut of the waffle case. The pilot's waffle case is Pip v. Ansel. This one starts as Ansel v. Marisol so that Pip is free to preside.
- `[TOWN]` and `[BRAND]` are Orchestrator placeholders.

---

```
                          KITCHEN COURT
                       90-second teaser

======================================================================
00:00   BEAT 1. COLD OPEN                                   (0 to 12)
======================================================================

INT. DELACROIX-HUANG KITCHEN, DUSK

No music. No title. Court is in session. We do not know that yet.

F1, TABLE WIDE. A red two-step kitchen stool stands at the head of
a long oval table. Whoever sits on it is cut off by the top of the
frame. At the stool's right hand, a rolling metal cart with a
four-slot chrome toaster. A small hand rests on the toaster lever.

ANSEL, 10, a clip-on tie over a T-shirt, sets a plate on the exact
center of the table. On the plate: one waffle. One bite gone.

                         ANSEL
          I'd like to enter into evidence the
          waffle.

                         PIP (O.S.)
          The waffle is entered.

                         RENATA (O.S.)
          Is that the waffle?

                         ANSEL
          It's a waffle. Somebody bit it. That's
          my whole case, and I have more.

F5, CART INSERT. The toast POPS.

                         PIP (O.S.)
          Time.

F3, CROSS-TABLE TWO-SHOT. Ansel's mouth is open on his next word.
It stays open. He looks at the toaster. He looks at the waffle. He
closes his mouth.

CUT TO BLACK.

======================================================================
00:12   BEAT 2. WORLD                                      (12 to 30)
======================================================================

EXT. THE PINWHEEL, DUSK, FROM ABOVE

A ring road with cul-de-sacs off it. From up here it is a pinwheel.
Porch lights come on one by one. Music enters, low.

EXT. SPOKE COURT, DEAD END, CONTINUOUS

The last split-level on the loop. A white van in the driveway,
side door open, folding chairs inside. On the van: HUANG PARTY
RENTAL, [TOWN].

INT. KITCHEN, CONTINUOUS

F4, DOCKET INSERT. The freezer door. A strip of black tape at the
top reads DOCKET in a kid's marker. A hand clips a slip under a
clothespin magnet. The slip: ANSEL v. MARISOL. RE: THE WAFFLE.

F1, TABLE WIDE. PIP, 6, a blue-stripe tea towel pinned across her
chest as a sash, drags the red stool from the fridge to the head of
the table. SCRAPE. She wheels the cart after it.

On the stairs, frame right, a brass whistle on a red lanyard rises
into view before a face does. TONI, 73, follows it up.

                         TONI
          Five dollars or five minutes. Anything
          bigger goes to the table. It's on the
          fridge.

RENATA, 44, reading glasses pushed up into her hair, climbs the
stool with the dignity of a person who has done this before and has
not made peace with it. She lifts a heavy old potato masher off a
nail by the range. Two taps on the cutting board.

                         PIP
          Kitchen Court is open. Feet on the
          floor. Hands on the table.

                         RENATA
          Who's hungry and who's mad?

                         ANSEL
          Mad.

MARISOL, 13, on the gallery bench across the table, a fat white
folder in front of her.

                         MARISOL
          Hungry.

======================================================================
00:30   BEAT 3. CHARACTER                                  (30 to 55)
======================================================================

F3. Marisol opens the folder. It is thick.

                         MARISOL
          I didn't bite the waffle. I have a
          folder that says so.

                         ANSEL
          Objection.

                         RENATA (O.S.)
          To what?

                         ANSEL
          She has a folder.

F2, STOOL SINGLE, low. Renata on the top step, a head above
everyone.

                         RENATA
          Overruled. Downtown I've never been
          reversed. In this kitchen I'm 0 and 4
          this week, and one of those was a
          first-grader.

                         PIP (O.S.)
               (not looking up from the
               toaster)
          Hi.

F1. The garage door swings wide. WENDELL, 45, backs in carrying a
stack of folding chairs taller than he is. He sets them down. He is
a little out of breath.

                         WENDELL
          Can we not split it? We could split
          it. It's a waffle. It comes with
          lines.

                         RENATA, TONI, MARISOL, ANSEL, PIP
          No.

Toni lifts the whistle. One short, low blow. Her fists roll over
each other.

                         TONI
          Traveling. You picked up the chairs
          and moved. That's traveling. Sit.

Wendell sits. In the chair with arms. Nobody had to tell him which
one.

======================================================================
00:55   BEAT 4. TURN                                       (55 to 75)
======================================================================

F3. Marisol closes the folder.

                         MARISOL
          I call one witness. I call Mom.

F1. A pause at the table. Renata climbs down from the stool, one
step at a time, and sits on a wooden stool with one short leg. It
tips. She steadies it. She puts her hand on a fat white binder
labeled HOUSE.

                         RENATA
          I promise to say what happened, not
          what it meant.

                         MARISOL
          Where were you at seven-fifteen this
          morning?

                         RENATA
               (beat)
          Alone. With the waffle.

                         MARISOL
          Let the record show.

                         RENATA
          It was a quality check. Let the record
          also show that I am very proud of you,
          and I would like a lawyer.

                         ANSEL
               (standing, grave)
          Amended. Everyone versus Mom.

                         RENATA
          Rule Four.

                         PIP
          Kid on the Stool.

F4, DOCKET INSERT. Beside the Docket, three name magnets on a ring:
MARISOL, ANSEL, PIP. A small hand turns the arrow magnet. It stops
on PIP.

F1. Everyone turns and looks at the smallest person in the room.
Pip looks at the stool. She unpins the tea towel, folds it once,
and sets it on the cart. Nobody says anything.

======================================================================
01:15   BEAT 5. BUTTON                                     (75 to 85)
======================================================================

F2, STOOL SINGLE, lower than before. Pip on the second step. The
fruit bowl hides half her face. She slides it aside with one hand.
Now we see her. She takes the masher in both hands.

                         PIP
          The fruit bowl is moved.

Two taps.

                         PIP (CONT'D)
          Kitchen Court is open. Who's hungry
          and who's mad?

F3, CROSS-TABLE TWO-SHOT. Renata on the gallery bench, the kids'
side, between Ansel and Marisol, glasses still up in her hair.

                         RENATA
          Both.

Pip slides the evidence waffle to the exact center of the table,
halfway between them. Not one inch closer to either.

======================================================================
01:25   BEAT 6. TITLE CARD                                 (85 to 90)
======================================================================

Black. The music resolves.

                         KITCHEN COURT
                             Anchor
                   Premieres Friday on [BRAND]
                 The account holder is a parent.

01:30   END
```

## The five tests

1. **Question test.** Three cold readers write "Will Pip rule against her mom?" The turn puts Mom in the case (00:55 to 01:15) and the Rota arrow on PIP. The button opens the trial and stops before any ruling. Nothing in the trailer answers it.
2. **Two-Laugh test.** Parent laugh, 00:38: "Downtown I've never been reversed. In this kitchen I'm 0 and 4 this week, and one of those was a first-grader." Kid laugh on sight, 00:09: the toast pops mid-sentence, Ansel's mouth stays open, Pip says "Time." Neither depends on the other. Spare parent laugh at 01:06: "It was a quality check. Let the record also show that I am very proud of you, and I would like a lawyer."
3. **Cold test.** First 12 seconds: a ten-year-old in a clip-on tie enters a bitten waffle into evidence at a kitchen table, a voice accepts it, a toaster cuts him off, and a child says "Time." No title, no music, no brand. The viewer asks "what is this?" because every object is ordinary and every word is procedure.
4. **Dialogue test.** Counted on the page at three words per second: cold open 9 of 12, world 11 of 18, character 23 of 25, turn 17 of 20, button 7 of 10, title 0 of 5. About 67 of 90 seconds, 74 percent, character speaking to character on screen. The only silent stretches are the aerial, the Rota insert, and the title card. Confirm with a stopwatch on the animatic.
5. **Standards test.** Title card carries the title, the slot, the premiere line, and the parent-account line, no child call to action. No courtroom object or word appears: the objects are a masher, a stool, a toaster, a freezer door, a wobbly stool, and a binder, all new expression per the bible appendix. Pop and whistle at or below dialogue peak. Marisol's cross-examination of her mother lands as affection: Renata answers with pride, cites Rule Four against her own interest, and steps down to sit with her children on the kids' side. She loses the Stool, not her dignity. No block trademark. No real person, brand, or show.

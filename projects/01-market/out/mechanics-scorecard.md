## How the H2 verdict enters the model

H2 is WEAKENED: the wrapper raises the floor and hit shows set the ceiling. The model does not score shows. It ranks which wrapper mechanics to keep. The H2 verdict enters as a ceiling on what any mechanic can claim: no mechanic is scored on ratings lift, only on recall (salience) and on whether a small digital studio can run it (reproducibility). The Reproduce band is deliberately narrow so that few mechanics reach it.
